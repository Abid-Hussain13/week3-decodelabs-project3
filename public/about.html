Why we write

The best engineering knowledge lives in short, honest essays — the kind you can read over a coffee and apply the same afternoon. Quire exists to publish those: practical, specific, and free of hype.

Every post here comes from real work. If we haven't shipped it, debugged it, or regretted it, we don't write about it.

How we work

Each draft is reviewed by at least one other engineer before it goes out. We correct mistakes openly and update posts when the landscape shifts.

Have a story worth telling? We're always open to guest pieces from people doing the work.
